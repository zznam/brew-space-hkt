Journey 01 · Customer Experience

Space Discovery, 3D Seat Picking & Live Session

Customer Role
Step 1.1 /customer/explore

Explore & 3D Floor Discovery

Real-time seat availability hero card, multi-amenity filter pills (Quiet, Outlet, Monitor, Dual-Desk), interactive 3D orbit canvas, and accessible seat list.

  • Hero availability banner with free/occupied KPIs & hourly rate range
  • Floating HUD camera controls on the 3D venue canvas
  • Seat Detail drawer with shareable deep link & package vs hourly selector
Step 1.2 /customer/explore?venue=&seat=

Venue Showcase & Deep-Linked Seat Picker

Detailed venue breakdown displaying zone cards, pricing tiers, Wi-Fi credentials, house rules, and query-parameter seat preselection.

  • Zone breakdown cards with seat counts and hourly rate ranges
  • Supports ?venue= & ?seat= deep links shared by teammates
  • Contextual banner confirming the preselected seat on arrival
Step 1.3 /customer/active-session

Live Workspace Session, Drink Customizer & Focus Timer

Active workspace dashboard combining live running bill breakdown, Pomodoro focus timer, customized drink ordering, loyalty point redemption, and table support requests.

  • Dark elevated live-bill banner with itemized seat + menu charges
  • Pomodoro Focus Timer with 15m / 25m / 45m presets
  • Expandable drink modifier drawer (milk, ice, sweetness, notes)
  • One-tap staff call buttons (Water, Power/Adapter, Clean Table, Bill Help)
Step 1.4 /customer/receipt

Digital Receipt, Refund Request & 5-Star Rating

Post-checkout itemized invoice showing VAT breakdown, loyalty points earned, self-serve issue/refund request tracking, and session rating.

  • Monospace invoice header with receipt ID, timestamps, and tax summary
  • Self-serve refund request card with live review status badge
  • Interactive 1–5 star rating form with feedback notes

Interactive Q&A assistant answering Wi-Fi, quiet seat picks, power outlet locations, pricing, and drink recommendations using live venue state.

Journey 03 · Merchant Operations

Live Floor Command Center, Counter POS & Kitchen Ticket Board

Merchant Role
Step 3.1 /merchant/live-floor

Live Floor Command Center & Seat Inspector

Real-time operational overview with occupancy rate, active tabs, accrued revenue KPIs, 3D floor map, contextual seat inspector, and active session table.

  • 4-tile KPI strip (Occupancy %, Occupied vs Free, Open Tabs, Accrued Revenue)
  • Side-by-side 3D floor map and contextual Seat Inspector panel
  • One-click Hourly or 3-Hour Study Pass check-in, quick drink add, and checkout
Step 3.2 /merchant/pos

Counter POS Terminal & Sold-Out Inventory Control

Fast barista point-of-sale terminal supporting walk-in or seat-linked orders, category filtering, live sold-out switches, and Cash / Online checkout.

  • Category filter pills with instant stock status badges and sold-out toggles
  • Seat assignment selector linking orders directly to floor desks
  • Order confirmation modal with receipt number and Ticket Board shortcut
Step 3.3 /merchant/tickets

Barista & Kitchen Ticket Board (KDS)

Fulfillment board tracking orders from Queued → Preparing → Ready → Served with elapsed timers and one-tap status progression.

  • Status filter tabs with live ticket counts per stage
  • Color-coded ticket cards showing seat badge, payment method, and line items
  • Single-tap progression (Start Preparing → Mark Ready → Mark Served)
Journey 04 · Spatial AI Studio

Multimodal AI 3D Venue Onboarding & Spatial Editor

Merchant Role
Step 4.1 /merchant/onboarding

AI 3D Venue Generator, Multi-Turn Chat & Spatial Studio

End-to-end onboarding wizard where cafe owners upload photos, videos, floorplans, or menus, generate a 3D venue with AI, refine it via multi-turn prompts, and publish live.

  • 4-step visual progress tracker (Upload → AI Refine → 3D Studio → Publish)
  • Multimodal media upload cards + optional runtime OpenAI API key input
  • Multi-turn conversational refinement with saved iteration snapshots
  • Interactive 3D room dimension, wall/floor palette, and furniture inspector
